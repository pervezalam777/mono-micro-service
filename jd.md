# Technical Lead / Team Lead - JavaScript Full Stack

We are seeking an experienced **Technical Lead / Team Lead** with strong expertise in JavaScript Full Stack development using Node.js, TypeScript, React, and Angular. The ideal candidate will lead a team of developers, drive architecture and design decisions, ensure engineering excellence, and deliver scalable, high-performance web applications.

This role requires a combination of technical leadership, hands-on development, mentoring, and stakeholder collaboration.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Technical Leadership
- Lead the design, development, and deployment of enterprise-grade web applications.
- Define and enforce best practices for software architecture, coding standards, security, performance, and scalability.
- Drive technical discussions, architecture reviews, and technology evaluations.
- Provide hands-on guidance and support to development teams.

### Full Stack Development
- Design and develop scalable backend services using Node.js and TypeScript.
- Build responsive and interactive user interfaces using React and/or Angular.
- Develop RESTful APIs, microservices, and event-driven solutions.
- Optimize application performance and ensure high availability.

### Team Management
- Lead and mentor a team of developers.
- Conduct code reviews and ensure adherence to quality standards.
- Support resource planning, task allocation, and delivery tracking.
- Foster a collaborative and high-performance engineering culture.

### Delivery & Stakeholder Management
- Collaborate with Product Owners, Architects, Business Analysts, QA, and DevOps teams.
- Translate business requirements into technical solutions.
- Manage project risks, dependencies, and technical debt.
- Ensure timely delivery of features with high quality.

## Required Skills & Experience

### Core Technologies
- 8+ years of software development experience.
- Strong expertise in JavaScript (ES6+) and TypeScript.
- Extensive experience with Node.js and backend development.
- Strong hands-on experience with React.js and Angular.
- Experience in designing and consuming REST APIs.

### Backend & Architecture
- Experience with microservices architecture.
- Strong understanding of asynchronous programming and event-driven architectures.
- Experience with API security, authentication, and authorization (OAuth2, JWT).
- Knowledge of system design and scalable application architecture.

### Databases
- Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQL Server.
- Experience with NoSQL databases such as MongoDB, DynamoDB, or Cosmos DB.

### Cloud & DevOps
- Experience with Azure, AWS, or GCP.
- Understanding of CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
- Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools.
